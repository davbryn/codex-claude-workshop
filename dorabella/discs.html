Dorabella cipher discs

Elgar's 24 symbols read as arcs of three nested discs. The sector tells you where the arc sits, and the number of arcs tells you which disc. With every disc at 0 the stack reproduces Elgar's key exactly. Turn or swap the discs to change the cipher.

Disc setting

“Ring” is the number of arcs the disc writes. “Step” turns that disc this many sectors after every letter, Alberti style. Leave it at 0 for a fixed key.

Encode

Decode and search

Write each symbol as the side its arcs sit on plus the arc count. W1 is a “c”, E2 is a “3”, S2 is an “ω”, N1 is an “n”.
